Hi, I'm Arend Peter Castelein. I'm a Software Engineer with 10 years of experience leading projects across the big tech, start up, and non-profit sectors. AI is changing our industry is changing fast, and like so many others I'm find myself excited but also a little intimidated.

The more I learn about the AI, the more I see it can allows us to become better engineers, and more code while also increase the quality.

This blog documents my journey staying at the cutting edge of the Software Engineering world, and discovering the bits that are most useful to the projects I work on every day. If you're also on that journey, then read on, and leave a comment. I'm eager to hear what resonates with you.
